Building micrograd from scratch, part 1

Following Karpathy's video line by line, and writing down every place I had to pause and rewind.

Notes on
The spelled-out intro to neural networks and backpropagation
Andrej Karpathy · YouTube · 2h 25m
watch ↗
The short version
  • A neural network is just a big math expression. Backprop is the chain rule, applied backwards through it.
  • micrograd fits in about 100 lines, small enough to understand all of it.
  • The part that tripped me up: gradients add up when a value is used twice.

Why I started here

I've shipped AI products for two years and I could not have told you, honestly, what loss.backward() does. That felt like a crack in the foundation, so I went back to the beginning.

The Value class

Everything starts with one tiny wrapper around a number that remembers how it was made:

class Value:
    def __init__(self, data, _children=(), _op=''):
        self.data = data
        self.grad = 0.0   # how much the output cares about me
        self._prev = set(_children)

That comment is mine, not Karpathy's. It's the sentence that finally made gradients click.
